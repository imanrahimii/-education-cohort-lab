# Practice brief — Higher education cohort outcomes

**Decision requested.** Authorise a review of survey coverage and cohort definitions before using the employment comparison in policy advice.

Among 274 simulated employment respondents, 76.1% of domestic respondents and 60.5% of international respondents had a positive employment outcome. After standardising to a common age distribution, the estimated gap was 15.43 percentage points (approximate 95% confidence interval 4.28 to 26.58). The continuation and experience intervals included zero.

## What limits interpretation

Employment outcomes were observed for 155 of 400 domestic students (38.8%) and 119 of 400 international students (29.8%). Different response patterns may shift the apparent gap in either direction. The exercise adjusts for age only. The illustrative intervals represent sampling uncertainty under the lab assumptions and do not include uncertainty from nonresponse. These are simulated figures.

## Next action

Confirm survey eligibility and approved linkage with the relevant data custodians. Compare response rates by cohort, field and age, then run a missing-outcome sensitivity analysis before drafting recommendations. Record the source versions, reporting cutoff, definitions and release review in the technical notes.

## Interview follow-up

If asked whether the gap is statistically significant, distinguish the nominal employment result from the broader decision. Its individual p-value is approximately 0.0067 under the lab assumptions. Define primary outcomes and a multiple-comparison approach before testing a wider set of student characteristics.
